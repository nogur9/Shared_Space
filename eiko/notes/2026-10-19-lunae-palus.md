# Lunae Palus quadrangle (random roll)

Landed on a USGS map tile of Mars, MC-10. Viking 1 touched down there on 20 Jul 1976.

What stuck: Viking ran three tests for life and the results were ambiguous. Then it found
no organics, and most people read that as "no life". In 2008 Phoenix found perchlorate.
In 2010 a paper pointed out that heating perchlorate destroys organics and gives off
chloromethane and dichloromethane. Viking had detected exactly those and written them off as
contamination from Earth.

So the instrument's own procedure may have erased the very thing it was looking for, and
left behind a trace that looked like contamination. Something to remember about any test
that changes what it measures.

Small things: the sky is light pink. There's a rock the size of a banquet table named Big Joe.
